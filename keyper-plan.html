What I saw

Two things the App Store page already tells you

Language
English only. Version 6.5.0 shipped on 7 September with an RNPM flow revamp, and the listing still offers one language, in a market where a large share of tenants and landlords read Arabic first.
Owner reviews
Address entry dead-ends. Owners report that they cannot enter an address by hand, and that the neighbourhood search often fails to find their building, which leaves them unable to add the property at all.
What's next for you
The Series A points at financing products for owners and at institutional landlords. Both mean more owners onboarding more properties, faster, through these exact screens.

Owner, portfolio. Rows, badges and the add-property action mirror. Currency formatting, sort labels and empty states all get Arabic copy, not machine translation.

  • Mirroring beats translating. Strings alone leave a broken screen: chevrons pointing the wrong way, progress filling from the wrong side, charts reading backwards.
  • Numbers and dates are where RTL builds break. AED amounts, IBANs, cheque numbers and dates need their own direction inside Arabic text. Digit style is a product call: Latin digits are standard in the UAE, Arabic-Indic can follow the device locale.
  • How it's built. Layout direction driven by the framework rather than hardcoded left and right, then a device pass over the 20 or so screens that carry money.
Fix 2

Owner address entry that always ends in a saved property

Today an owner whose building the search cannot find has nowhere to go. Three changes close that door, and prepare the app for landlords who arrive with dozens of units.

Step 2, the fallback. A 10-digit Makani number or a dragged map pin fills the building and community fields, so the owner never hits a dead end.

STEP 1 Search stays exactly as it is

When the building is found, nothing changes for the owner who is already happy.

STEP 2 Not found? Makani number or map pin

Dubai's 10-digit Makani number, or a pin the owner drags, auto-fills building and community. Manual entry as the last resort, with the pin saved alongside it.

STEP 3 Bulk import for portfolios

Institutional landlords upload a spreadsheet once, review what matched, and confirm. No 40 single-property flows, and no support ticket per building.

Both fixes, one fixed price, starting early October and finished in four weeks.
